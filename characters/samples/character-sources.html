The Red Flag Theory · where to get a real cast

Ready-made characters, side by side

Searched 2026-11-03. Prices and licence terms are quoted from each seller's own page; anything not seen is marked unknown. Pictures load straight from the seller's site.

My pick to test first: ThreeDee One's cartoon characters (Superhive). It's the only artist-made 3D cast I found with modern everyday clothes, a face rig with mouth shapes for lip sync, and native Blender files. You'd buy 2 or 3 single characters (about $34–44 each on the current sale) once their licence is confirmed, and I'd run them through the same talk and sit test. Free side test, at the same time: one toned-down VRoid character you make in VRoid Studio. VRoid characters already include lip-sync mouth shapes, and its terms allow commercial use.

What decides it is the face. Lip sync and expressions need mouth and face shapes on the character. Human Generator has them but is photoreal. AutoCharacters has the best wardrobe and is stylized, but its page says nothing about the face. My move: ask the AutoCharacters seller (Superhive "Have questions about this product?" button) whether the face has expression or mouth shape keys, meaning preset face shapes. Check these yourself (login walls or bot blocks)

  • Patreon blocks bots and hides the back catalogue. Search "stylized character blender", then also "rigged cartoon character", "toon character commercial". Look for: a post list of past characters, "commercial use" in the tier text, and Blender or FBX files. Quaternius tiers: patreon.com/quaternius/membership.
  • ThreeDee One licence: on any product page, open the "License" tab next to the buy button, or email samuel@threedee.design (shown on their page) and ask "Can I use renders in monetised YouTube videos?"
  • Fab.com, CGTrader, ArtStation (search "stylized character rigged casual"): filter to "Standard licence" and rigged characters with a face rig.
  • Envato Elements / Motion Array "character animation kit" (2D After Effects rigs): these need a login. They're probably app-locked too.
