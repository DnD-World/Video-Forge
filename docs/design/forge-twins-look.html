Two Forges, one family

Both apps keep the same bones. Each gets its own colour and its own texture, so with both open you know which is which without hunting for the logo. Video Forge is the same in every option: mint, with a faint moving film grain like footage. Only Image Forge's colour changes between A, B and C. Its texture is always frosted glass with a slow light sheen, like a lens.
