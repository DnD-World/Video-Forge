# Make a video, in plain words

Four steps, left to right: **Cast, Script, Look, Make.**

## 1. Cast: who is talking
- Press **+** to add a character. Pick a face from the list: ready-made, made in Image Forge, or your own.
- Hover over a face and it talks to you.
- Don't see the one you want? **Order it from Image Forge**. It opens Image Forge, makes the face, and it shows up here by itself.
- Give each character a voice.
- **How much they gesture** decides how much they wave their hands. Zero means hands stay still. Hands move on their own: a question gets a shrug, "this" or "here" gets a point.

## 2. Script: what they say
- Write one line per speaker: `Name: what they say`.
- Add feelings or actions in square brackets: `[laughs]`, `[waves]`, `[points]`, `[shrugs]`, `[nods]`.
- Greek without accent marks is fine. The app adds them before the voice speaks. If a word still sounds wrong, add it to **Settings → Say it like this**.

## 3. Look: how it looks
- Pick the frame, the moving sound bars, the subtitles and the background.
- Everything you see in the preview is what you get.

## 4. Make: get the file
- Press **Render video**.
- **Also as**: tick Tall, Square, Portrait or Wide to get more shapes from the same script (for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).
- **Also in English / Greek**: get a second copy in the other language. The app translates the script. Same characters, matching subtitles.
- **Made with AI · Video Forge**: a small label on the picture. It's on by default. Untick it if you don't want it.
- When it finishes you get the video plus subtitle files (`.srt` and `.vtt`) next to it.
- **Many videos**: paste a Google Sheet link (shared "anyone with the link") with one row per video. The app makes them one after another into a folder named after the sheet.

## Stuck?
Hover over any button or box. A little bubble tells you what it does.
